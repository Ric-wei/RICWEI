Your Donation Changes Lives

RICWEI relies on the generosity of individuals, corporate partners, and grants to deliver life-changing programs. Every naira you give directly empowers a rural woman, child, or adolescent.

₦15,000 sponsors a vocational training session for 5 women
₦50,000 supports a community maternal health outreach
₦100,000 funds a complete scholarship for one indigent pupil for a year
