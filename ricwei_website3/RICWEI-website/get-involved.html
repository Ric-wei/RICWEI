Institutional Collaboration

Partner With RICWEI

One of our constitutional objectives is to collaborate with faith-based institutions, government agencies, local organizations, and international development partners to implement sustainable empowerment programs. If your organization shares our mission, we would welcome the conversation.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RICWEI a registered organization?
Yes. Rural Impact for Children and Women Empowerment Initiative was incorporated on 21 March 2026 under the Companies and Allied Matters Act, CAP C20 LFN 2004, as an Incorporated Trustee with registration number CAC/IT/9430700.
Where does RICWEI work?
Our programs are based in rural communities in Benue State, Nigeria, operating from our office in the Makurdi/Aliade area, with plans to extend to more rural communities as programs grow.
How can I volunteer with RICWEI?
Use the volunteer form on this page to tell us which program area interests you and your availability. Our team will reach out to discuss how your skills and time can be put to use.
How can I donate?
You can give online through our donate page, or by direct bank transfer using the account details listed there. Please send proof of payment to our email so we can send you an acknowledgment.
Does RICWEI partner with other organizations?
Yes. One of our constitutional objectives is to collaborate with faith-based institutions, government agencies, local organizations, and international development partners to implement sustainable empowerment programs.
Is my donation tax deductible?
This depends on your country of residence and current Nigerian tax regulations for Incorporated Trustees. Please contact us directly so we can guide you and provide the documentation you may need.
