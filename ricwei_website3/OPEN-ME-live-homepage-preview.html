Welcome to RICWEI

Together We Can Create Lasting Change

Rural Impact for Children and Women Empowerment Initiative (RICWEI) is a not-for-profit, non-political organization incorporated on 21 March 2026 under the Companies and Allied Matters Act, CAP C20 LFN 2004. We work to promote the economic empowerment of rural women and adolescent girls, improve access to quality education and healthcare, and advocate for policies that advance gender equality and social inclusion.

  • Vocational training & entrepreneurship development
  • Maternal & child health interventions
  • Scholarships for indigent pupils
  • Community leadership & rights-based education

What We Stand For

Our Mission, Vision & Values

Driven by purpose, guided by integrity, and powered by community.

Our Mission

To empower rural women, adolescents, and children through vocational training, entrepreneurship development, education, healthcare access, and policy advocacy that promotes gender equality and social inclusion.

Our Vision

A Nigeria where every rural woman and child lives with dignity, has access to quality education and healthcare, and enjoys equal opportunity to thrive.

Our Values

Integrity, Inclusion, Empowerment, Sustainability, Collaboration, Respect for Human Dignity.

Seven interconnected focus areas drive our work across rural Nigerian communities.

Women Economic Empowerment Program

Skills acquisition in tailoring, catering, soap-making, and beadwork, with micro-grants and cooperative formation to sustain livelihoods.

End Harmful Practices Campaign

Community dialogues, sensitization campaigns, and rights-based education to end child marriage, GBV, and inheritance denial.
